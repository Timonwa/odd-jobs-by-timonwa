Writing is the hard part. Once an article exists, it's a mine you can keep coming back to. Here's how I turn one piece into a week of content without ever facing a blank page.

## Start with the article

Publish the long-form piece first — it's the source everything else draws from.

## Break it into posts

Run it through the [Article to Social Posts](https://tools.timonwa.com/article-to-social-posts) tool to get platform-shaped drafts, then space them across the week. One idea per post beats cramming everything in.

## Pull the metadata

Use [Article to SEO Meta](https://tools.timonwa.com/article-to-seo-meta) so the piece is set up to be found — title and description sized to Google's limits.

## Send the highlight

Take the single best point and write a short email around it for the newsletter. People who missed the article still get the value.

## Reuse the offcuts

The lines that didn't make the final draft? Those are quote graphics and one-liners. Nothing goes to waste.

One article, a week of content, zero blank pages.
