Most weeks go sideways not because we run out of time, but because we never decided what the week was _for_. A short planning ritual fixes that. Here's the 20-minute version I actually stick to.

## 1. Brain-dump (5 min)

Get everything out of your head and into one list — tasks, errands, half-ideas, the lot. Don't organise yet; just capture.

## 2. Pick three outcomes (5 min)

From the dump, choose the **three** things that would make the week a win. Not thirty. Three. Everything else is optional.

## 3. Block the time (5 min)

Put those three outcomes on the calendar as real blocks. If it doesn't have a time, it won't happen.

## 4. Clear the runway (5 min)

Scan for anything that could derail the blocks — a reply you owe, a file you need — and handle or schedule it now.

That's it. Twenty minutes on a Sunday (or Monday morning) buys you a week that mostly runs itself.
