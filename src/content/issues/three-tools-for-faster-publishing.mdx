You finished the draft. Now comes the part nobody enjoys: reformatting it for every platform. Here's the workflow I use.

## 1. Social posts

Paste the article (or its URL) into **Article to Social Posts**. Pick your platforms and tone, and you get ready-to-post drafts — including an X thread — that you can tweak and copy.

## 2. SEO metadata

Run the same article through **Article to SEO Meta** for title and description variations sized to Google's display limits, with your primary keyword worked in.

## 3. A clean slug

Drop the headline into the **Slug Generator** for a tidy, URL-safe slug.

Three tools, a few minutes, and the draft is ready to ship everywhere. That's the whole point.
