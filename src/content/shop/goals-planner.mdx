The **Yearly Goals Planner & Tracker Notion Template** is built for anyone who wants to stay consistent with their goals—not just at the beginning of the year, but throughout the year.

Instead of letting resolutions fade, this template gives you a simple structure for planning, tracking, reviewing, and celebrating your growth. You can categorize your goals, assign priorities, track milestones, complete monthly or quarterly check-ins, and reflect at the end of the year with guided prompts.

Whether your goals are personal, academic, financial, or professional, this system helps you stay grounded and intentional—without feeling overwhelmed.

## Key features

- **Goal setting & tracking** — organize goals into categories, assign priorities, break them into milestones, and choose which quarter to focus on each one.
- **Milestones tracker** — record important events, wins, setbacks, and breakthroughs so nothing gets forgotten.
- **Periodic reviews** — monthly review pages help you reflect on progress and clarify next steps.
- **Quarterly progress view** — track wins and challenges every quarter to see how your year is unfolding.
- **Planning guide** — define your yearly theme, set clear intentions, and map out actionable milestones.
- **Guiding principles** — add affirmations, quotes, and personal rules to stay motivated.
- **Task tracker** — manage day-to-day tasks with priorities and progress tracking.
- **End-of-year review** — a structured reflection to help you understand your growth and celebrate the year.

## Who is this planner for?
