- **Use the software** under the AGPL, including running your own instance.

- **Run a fork or modified version under the same name or logo**, or any name/branding confusingly similar to it. Rename and rebrand your instance.

If you fork the code (which you're welcome to do under the AGPL), please give your version its own name and identity.
